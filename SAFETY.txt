open-sds1000cml — SAFETY / USE AT YOUR OWN RISK
================================================

This is EXPERIMENTAL clean-room replacement firmware for the Siglent SDS1000CML+
series oscilloscope (developed and validated on the SDS1102CML+).

- It TAKES OVER the instrument. Booting a scope with this USB stick inserted will,
  a couple of seconds after power-on, stop the factory application and run this
  firmware instead. It will take over WHATEVER SDS1000CML+ you boot it in.
- It drives the real hardware (relays, gain/offset DACs, the memory-mapped
  acquisition bus). A bug can wedge the acquisition engine or leave the front end
  in an unexpected state.
- It is for the SDS1000CML+ SERIES ONLY. Do not run it on other hardware.
- Its network services (web UI on port 8080, SCPI, the agent's control port 5900)
  have NO AUTHENTICATION; port 5900 runs commands on the scope as root. Use it on
  a trusted network only.
- There is NO WARRANTY. You are responsible for your instrument. See LICENSE (MIT).

Recovery: remove the USB stick and power-cycle to boot the factory firmware again.
With the stick in, the scope runs this firmware at every boot.

Project + full docs: https://github.com/labeth/open-sds1000cml
